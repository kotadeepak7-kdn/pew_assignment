ROLE 01

IT Company Recruiter

Hiring communication for a technology company.

📧 Professional Email

Subject: Invitation to Technical Interview – Software Engineer Position
To: Shortlisted Candidate

Dear Candidate,

Thank you for applying for the Software Engineer position at NovaTech Solutions. We are pleased to inform you that your application has been shortlisted for the next stage of our selection process. We appreciate the time you have invested in your application.

Your technical interview will focus on problem-solving, programming fundamentals and your approach to real-world software development. Please confirm your preferred interview slot through the recruitment portal by the stated deadline. If you have any questions, please reply to this email and our team will assist you.

If you require any reasonable assistance during the interview process, please let us know in advance.

We look forward to speaking with you.

Regards,
Talent Acquisition Team
NovaTech Solutions

ROLE 02

Chief Laughing Officer

Employee wellbeing and positive workplace communication.

📧 Professional Email

Subject: Building a Healthier and Happier Workplace
To: All Employees

Hello Team,

A healthy workplace is built through small, consistent actions. This month, we are introducing short wellbeing activities designed to encourage connection, relaxation and positive communication. Everyone is welcome to participate, and no one is expected to join an activity that does not suit them.

You can join the weekly “10-Minute Recharge”, team laughter sessions and optional feedback circles. Participation is voluntary, and respectful feedback is always welcome.

Let us make wellbeing part of the way we work—not an extra task.

Warm regards,
Chief Laughing Officer
People & Culture Team

ROLE 03

Football Coach

Team preparation, performance and match-day communication.

📧 Professional Email

Subject: Match Preparation Plan – Saturday Fixture
To: First Team Squad

Team,

Saturday’s match requires disciplined preparation and clear communication. Please arrive 90 minutes before kickoff for the warm-up and tactical briefing. If you have a genuine difficulty with the timing, inform the coaching team as early as possible.

Our focus areas are defensive organization, quick transitions and accurate passing. Please review the match plan and raise any questions during tomorrow’s training session. During the match, communicate clearly, support teammates and avoid unnecessary criticism.

Stay focused, support one another and communicate respectfully on and off the field.

Regards,
Head Coach

ROLE 04

Stock Broker

Clear, responsible and data-driven market communication.

📧 Professional Email

Subject: Weekly Market Update – Key Movements and Risk Reminder
To: Client

Dear Client,

Please find below a brief summary of this week’s portfolio review. Market movements were influenced by company results, economic indicators and changes in investor sentiment. The information is provided for discussion and should be considered alongside your own financial objectives.

Your portfolio should be reviewed against your investment objectives, time horizon and risk tolerance. Historical performance does not guarantee future returns, and no particular outcome is guaranteed. Please reply if you would like to discuss the information further.

Please contact us if you would like to discuss diversification or your current allocation.

Regards,
Client Advisory Desk
ABC Securities
